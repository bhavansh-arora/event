Our story

It started with a cousin's shaadi.

In 2012, our founder planned her cousin's wedding when the family's planner backed out two weeks before the date. The Haldi, Sangeet and Pheras all went off perfectly, and three relatives asked her to plan theirs.

Fourteen years later, Utsava is a team of 35 planners, designers and on-ground crew, working with 300+ trusted vendors across India. We still work the way we did at that first wedding: one family at a time, with care, honesty and a lot of attention to detail.
