FAQ

Aapke sawaal, hamare jawaab.

Still have questions? Send us a message or call +91 98765 43210.

How early should we book for a wedding?

For Nov–Feb muhurat dates, book 6–9 months ahead because the best venues and vendors fill fast. For single functions like a Haldi or birthday, 3–4 weeks is usually enough, and we've done beautiful events in 7 days too.

Do you plan only weddings or other events too?

Everything. We do Roka, Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet, Shaadi and Reception, plus birthdays, anniversaries, baby showers, griha pravesh, Diwali and Holi parties, Dandiya nights and corporate events.

Can we use our own caterer, pandit-ji or decorator?

Of course. We happily work with your family vendors and fill any gaps from our network of 300+ trusted vendors.

How does payment work?

A small booking amount locks your date. The rest is paid in easy instalments linked to milestones. You get a proper GST invoice and a clear, itemised budget, with no hidden charges.

Do you handle outstation guests and destination weddings?

Yes. We manage hotel blocks, airport and railway pickups, a hospitality desk, welcome hampers and full itineraries for Udaipur, Jaipur, Goa, Jim Corbett, Kerala and more.
